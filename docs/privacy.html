Privacy Policy

Focus Block · Last updated: October 29, 2026

Focus Block is a Chrome extension that blocks the websites you choose so you can stay focused. This policy explains what data it handles.

Your blocked sites stay on your computer

The list of sites you block is stored only in your browser (chrome.storage.local). It is never sent to our servers or to anyone else. Focus Block does not read, collect or store your browsing history or the content of the pages you visit.

Account

An account is only needed to subscribe to Premium. When you create one, we store your email address and a securely hashed password with our authentication provider, Supabase. We use your email only to sign you in, confirm your account and send password reset links.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card details. We keep only your Stripe customer ID and your subscription status (for example active, canceled and the renewal date) to unlock Premium features.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data with third parties, other than the providers above that run the service.
  • We don't use analytics, ads or tracking.
  • We don't use your data for any purpose unrelated to the extension's single purpose: blocking distracting websites.

Deleting your data

Removing the extension deletes your blocked site list. To delete your account and subscription record, cancel your subscription and email us at fernandu.contact@gmail.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy: fernandu.contact@gmail.com.

O Focus Block é uma extensão do Chrome que bloqueia os sites que você escolher para você manter o foco. Esta política explica quais dados ela usa.

Seus sites bloqueados ficam no seu computador

A lista de sites que você bloqueia fica salva só no seu navegador (chrome.storage.local). Ela nunca é enviada para os nossos servidores nem para ninguém. O Focus Block não lê, coleta nem guarda seu histórico de navegação ou o conteúdo das páginas que você visita.
